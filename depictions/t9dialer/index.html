T9Dialer

Korean initial-consonant T9 search for the Phone app.

Tested on iOS 16–18 in Rootless and RootHide environments. The package installs on iOS 26, but runtime behavior has not been tested.

Features

  • Korean and English contact search with optional phone-number matching.
  • Inline swipe and result-sheet interfaces.
  • Speed dial on keys 2–9, key haptics, and configurable highlight color.
  • Toggleable Korean TelephonyUI-9/10 keypad images with original-image restoration.
  • Required color-picker dependency bundled into one package.

Credits
